We were at this Cafe Coffee Day (CCD) in Trivandrum. I had an account opening to get done and was planning on leaving to visit the bank. Rohan who was with us asked me to just have the bank rep come to CCD.

It was instinctive for me to go to the bank. It was instinctive for Rohan to have the bank rep come to where you are. Our instincts were a function of our upbringing. Of mine in a middle class family and of his in a business family.
